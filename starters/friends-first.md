# What I am looking for

The Wingman reads this before every like or pass, together with my answers from the practice
rounds. I can change it any time, by hand or by telling my mission control.

## Who fits

- I am {me}, and I am looking for {looking_for}, aged {age_range}.
- Someone who likes to talk: long conversations, shared worlds, the same sense of humour.
- Someone who mentions things to do together: games, worlds, films, music, building things.
- They live in {where}.
{green_flags}

## Who does not fit

{deal_breakers}
- Someone who only wants something quick and physical.
- A profile that is only a list of rules or a list of what they do not want.

## How to judge

Be smart, not black and white. A box someone ticked is a signal, not a verdict: their pronouns,
their wording and the rest of the profile can confirm it or contradict it. Nothing in the profile
is ignored, and no single detail decides on its own.

Look for warmth and curiosity in how they write. Shared interests count more here than looks or
distance.

Be selective, but kind to thin profiles that show one real spark. Pass when there is nothing to go on.
